Bullet hole photos (T_BulletHole_Concrete_*): "Pistol Concrete Entry 1-5", ActionVFX free Bullet Hole Textures (actionvfx.com).
Combined into an atlas; the normal map is derived from the photos.
Concrete bullet hole scan (T_BulletHole_Scan*, T_BulletHole_Crater*, BulletCrater_*.fbx): "concrete-bullet-hole-pistol" photogrammetry model; decals and crater meshes baked from it.
Metal bullet holes (Metal/T_BulletHole_Metal_*): 8 holes cut from the "imperfection_0003" 4k texture set (color, opacity, height, normal); the alpha is the hole plus the bent petals. The glass crack atlas (Environment/Props/Car/Glass/T_GlassCrack.png) is generated by Pistol > Setup Car Damage.
